See the assumptions behind this planning tool

Google Search uses CPC assumptions of JMD 80–300 and CTR of 3–8%. YouTube uses CPM of JMD 250–900; Display JMD 200–800; Meta JMD 250–1,000. These are illustrative constants, not verified market benchmarks. Reach also depends on assumed frequency. Do not use these ranges as a performance promise or a quote.
