Common questions before we begin
Can I start with an idea or a business challenge?

Yes. Creative Strategy & Concept Development helps find the story, picture the direction and prepare a brief before production. It can be a standalone project or part of ongoing support.

What does ongoing support include?

We agree the priorities, deliverables, capacity and reporting that fit your business. It can bring planning, production, campaigns and platform support together.

Can I book paid advertising separately?

Yes. Google or Meta Ads management can be a focused engagement using your existing website. Website changes and advertising spend are scoped separately.

Can I start with a specific project?

Yes. Content production, website work, learning materials and other creative projects can be delivered to an agreed brief.

How are fees agreed?

We discuss what you need, the work involved and your timing, then agree the scope and quote before starting.

Can we work together outside Jamaica?

Yes. Remote support is available internationally. We agree communication and meeting times at the start.
