# Creative strategy refinement

## Working direction

Incremental preview based on published commit `f5708f2`, on `preview/creative-strategy`. Production publication requires a new explicit instruction under the user's latest brief.

Primary design mode: Premium Commercial. Editorial is used for the creative-process demonstration. The business objective is to help owners bring an unfinished idea, launch or communication challenge to Kyle before investing in production. The conversion is an enquiry with Creative Strategy & Concept Development selected. The emotional target is thoughtful, practical and distinctive; avoid vague artistic mystique.

Keep the existing violet, warm paper, gold and mint tokens, Outfit display type, readable system body text, authentic portrait, portfolio, enquiry tools and brand-led sharing image. The first-screen positioning stays intact. Add ideation as the beginning of the connected capability pathway, with a matching outline brain icon and restrained gold pulse.

The memorable moment is an original café example that moves from an everyday observation to visual direction and a production brief. It is clearly labelled illustrative, with no client or campaign claim. Borrow the pacing of a studio sketchbook and the clarity of the site's existing interactive process; do not add generic cards or external animation libraries.

Six hero controls use a connected serpentine 3×2 grid where six columns would become cramped. The new editorial section replaces the existing process section's default footprint. Preserve the wider four-stage process and the net scene inside accessible disclosures, so the homepage grows only where the new offer earns space.

Creative Strategy stays within Creative & Digital Projects, is also offered within Marketing Partner, and is available independently. Update the existing form, guide, supporting service catalog, Project Match and assistant topic mapping. Keep the number of discovery questions unchanged.

No publication permission or approved materials were supplied for the employment-related concept mentioned in the private brief. Its preparation is kept outside this public checkout. No names, artwork, campaign details or hidden case markup from that concept will enter public files or client-side bundles.
