# Marketing partner preview

User source: the attached Kahnec Hub Website Repositioning & UX/UI Upgrade brief. This revision is a preview; production publication requires explicit authorisation under that brief.

## Direction

Primary mode: Premium Commercial, with Editorial composition for the person and portfolio. The emotional target is confident, creative and approachable. Avoid the overly neutral treatment of the previous version.

Core idea: connected marketing capabilities, led by one accountable person. The opening message is “Your marketing team, without the overhead.” Ongoing Marketing Support leads; Paid Ads & Growth and Creative & Digital Projects remain independently available. The primary conversion is a marketing conversation, with portfolio exploration and the existing booking/WhatsApp paths alongside it.

Use warm paper, deep Kahnec violet, golden connectors and restrained mint. Outfit gives display typography its brand character; body copy and controls remain comfortably readable. Meaningful process graphics replace free-floating decoration. Keep the existing native press feedback and interruptible selected surfaces.

## Audit and preservation

Preserve the canonical URLs, existing insights, service pages, sample-site explorer, website/learning/AI/advertising illustrations, custom-project enquiry, authentic Kyle photography and the net scene. Keep Formspree, Calendly, email, WhatsApp, current analytics configuration, validation and draft-editing behaviour. Existing employment/prototype attribution stays visible.

Professional work is quoted by scope. Remove fixed fee displays from the preview and replace rigid monthly packages with agreed engagement models. Retain the existing campaign estimator's visitor-controlled advertising-budget inputs; these are media-spend scenarios, not professional fees.
