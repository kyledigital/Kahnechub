Google Ads & YouTube

Who does their marketing?

Someone saw an ad and wanted to know who made it. Would they remember the business a week later, when they actually needed it?

When the customer needs you

Think of a homeowner replacing a water heater. A month ago, they might have watched a local installer explain how a solar heater works. Today, with cold water coming out of the shower, they’re looking for someone who can fit one.

Google Search Ads

“Solar water heater Jamaica” tells you what someone is looking for. Send them to a page with the models you install, the areas you cover and a way to ask for a quote.

YouTube

Film the installer answering a question they hear on the job. A customer who’s still weighing up solar can see the equipment and meet the person who fits it.

Try the enquiry yourself

Open the page on your phone. Does the headline match the ad? Can you see the product, tell what’s included and reach someone by phone, WhatsApp or the enquiry form?

Agree what you’ll measure before paying for traffic. A video view tells you someone watched. A quote request tells you someone got in touch. Give the campaign enough time and budget to see which enquiries it brings in.
