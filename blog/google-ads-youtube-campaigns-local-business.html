The Ad That Made Someone Ask: “Who Does Their Marketing?”

Good digital marketing should not just run in the background. It should make the right people notice your business, remember your message, and take action.

Book a Marketing Audit View Services

Sometimes the best proof that a campaign is working is not only a click, a form fill, or a report. Sometimes it is when someone sees your ad, remembers it, and asks, “Who is doing their marketing?”

That moment matters because it means the campaign did more than appear on a screen. It created attention.

For small and medium-sized businesses, that kind of attention is valuable. It can help people recognise your name, understand your offer, and feel more confident when they are ready to enquire.

“Sometimes the best sign of a campaign working is when someone asks who made it.”

Why attention matters before the sale

People often buy from businesses they remember. They may not act the first time they see your brand, especially if the product or service needs trust, comparison, or a bigger decision.

This is where digital marketing can do more than chase instant clicks. YouTube ads can build familiarity because people see the business, hear the message, and understand the offer. Google Search Ads can then capture people when they are closer to taking action.

Google Search Ads

Capture demand

Google Search Ads help your business show up when people are already looking for what you sell.

  • Emergency repair service near me
  • Home medical equipment in Jamaica
  • Best contractor in Kingston
  • Solar water heater Jamaica
  • Business website designer Jamaica

These are high-intent searches because the person already has a need.

YouTube Campaigns

Build trust

YouTube ads are useful because people can see the business, hear the message, and understand the offer before they click.

  • Product explainer
  • Service introduction
  • Founder message
  • Customer problem and solution
  • Location or showroom walkthrough
  • Offer-based campaign
  • Educational tip video

The best campaigns use both

Search Ads help people find you when they are ready. YouTube helps people remember you before they search.

Together, they can create stronger brand recall and better leads. Someone may first notice your business on YouTube, then later search for your service on Google when the need becomes urgent.

What a local business needs before running ads

Clear offer Simple landing page Working contact form WhatsApp or phone link Basic tracking Strong headline Good product or service photos Short video or simple ad creative Budget that can collect enough data

Common mistake businesses make

A common mistake is boosting random posts without a plan. Another is sending traffic to a weak website, then wondering why the leads are poor.

Businesses also lose useful information when they do not track leads, use one ad for everyone, stop campaigns before enough data is collected, or focus only on views instead of business outcomes.
