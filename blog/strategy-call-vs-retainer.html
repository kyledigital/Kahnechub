When a strategy call is better than a retainer.

Ongoing marketing support is useful when the direction is clear. If the next move is still foggy, a focused strategy call can be the better first step.

Use a call when the question is still unclear.

If you are unsure whether to fix ads, rebuild a landing page, create content, or pause and rethink the offer, jumping into a retainer can create more motion than progress.

Use a project when the deliverable is clear.

If you already know you need a pitch deck, content kit, campaign setup, or landing page, a defined project may be more efficient than a broad monthly arrangement.

Use monthly support when consistency is the bottleneck.

A retainer makes sense when the strategy is clear and the business needs regular execution, reporting, content, ad optimisation, and accountability.

Useful question: Do you need a decision, a deliverable, or consistent execution?

The right starting point should reduce confusion. Sometimes that is one focused call before anything larger is scoped.
