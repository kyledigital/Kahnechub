What I’ll report

We agree what can be tracked before the campaign starts. Where tracking and your records support it, I’ll report calls, WhatsApp enquiries and bookings alongside spend.

Clicks on contact buttons are counted separately from completed enquiries. I use what we can measure to review the campaign and decide what to adjust.

Google Search uses CPC assumptions of JMD 80–300 and CTR of 3–8%. YouTube uses CPM of JMD 250–900; Display JMD 200–800; Meta JMD 250–1,000. These are illustrative constants, not verified market benchmarks. Reach also depends on assumed frequency. Do not use these ranges as a performance promise or a quote.
