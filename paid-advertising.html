How I run your campaign

  1. Set up

    Agree the offer and audience, check the account and test the enquiry tracking.

  2. Launch

    Match the creative and keywords to the landing page before the budget goes live.

  3. Test

    Review search terms, placements and creative. Change what the evidence supports.

  4. Review

    See spend, results and the next decision together. Confirmed enquiries are identified separately.

These are illustrative constants, not verified market benchmarks. Reach also depends on assumed frequency. Do not use these ranges as a performance promise or a quote.
