Before we talk

A few practical questions.

Is the discovery call a free audit?

No. It is a brief conversation to understand your needs and assess fit. A professional audit or strategy session is a separately agreed paid engagement.

Do I need both Google Ads and Meta Ads?

Not necessarily. The right channels depend on your service, audience, search demand, budget and conversion path. We can discuss which approach fits.

Does management include my advertising spend?

No. Your media budget pays for advertising on the platforms. Kahnec Hub’s professional fees cover the agreed work and are quoted separately.

Can we work together outside Jamaica?

Yes. I work remotely with international service businesses. We agree scope, communication and meeting times before the engagement.

Can I get help with a specific project?

Yes. Supporting services include strategy sessions, campaign assets, presentation decks and landing pages, with a defined scope.
