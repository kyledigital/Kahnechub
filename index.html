Meta and Google Ads

Meta and Google Ads,
planned and managed.

I plan the campaign, manage the ads and work out what content is needed. We can start with your existing website or another agreed enquiry route.

  • Campaign strategy, audiences and budgets
  • Meta or Google Ads setup, management and testing
  • Ad copy, photos or video suited to the campaign
  • Reporting on enquiries and bookings where tracking allows
Discuss paid advertising

I quote the work separately. Advertising spend is additional.

What I’ll report

We agree what can be tracked before the campaign starts. Where tracking and your records support it, I’ll report calls, WhatsApp enquiries and bookings alongside spend.

Clicks on contact buttons are counted separately from completed enquiries. I use what we can measure to review the campaign and decide what to adjust.

Yes. I can review how your site supports your business goals and identify improvements. We agree whether targeted updates or a new build make sense.
