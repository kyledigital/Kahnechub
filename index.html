Paid advertising

Meta Ads & Google Ads,
managed with a plan.

I set up the accounts, build the campaigns and manage them as they run. Each ad leads to a website or landing page built for the enquiry, and you get a report you can read.

Discuss Meta or Google Ads

How a campaign is run

  1. 01

    Set-up

    Account structure, audiences and keywords. Conversion tracking is checked before the budget goes live.

  2. 02

    Management

    Budgets, search terms and placements are reviewed while the campaign runs. Each change is explained.

  3. 03

    Creative testing

    New graphics and video come from the same studio, and are tested against the ads already running.

  4. 04

    Measurement

    Every number comes with its reporting period and what was counted. Impressions are not people reached. A click on a contact button is not an enquiry.
